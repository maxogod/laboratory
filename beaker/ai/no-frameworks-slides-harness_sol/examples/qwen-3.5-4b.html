The Nether Portal

  • Requirements: 8 Cobblestone + 4 Glowstone in a T shape
  • Location: Anywhere on Bedrock or Lava blocks
  • Effect: Creates teleportation portal with purple light

Crafting the Portal

  • Recipe: 3 rows of blocks, central row has Glowstone surrounded by Cobblestone
  • Placement: Set blocks in air (not on top of other blocks)
  • Lighting: Use torches to illuminate your crafting area!

Finding Strongholds

  • Spawn Chance: Approximately 1 in every 5-6 worlds
  • Navigation: Follow beds to Nether, then use Dragon's Breath or Wither Skulls
  • Tips: Stay at bottom of world (bedrock level) when navigating
